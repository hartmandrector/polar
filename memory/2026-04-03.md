## Key Domain Knowledge from Hartman
- **GPS accel limited to ±2g** — opening shock (2-3g) may be clipped; rely on airspeed deceleration not just accel magnitude
- **PC toss to line stretch is ~1 second**, not 2-4s as initially estimated. Reach+grab is another ~1s before that
- **Post-line-stretch sub-phases**: max AoA (canopy forces dominant, trust gauge) → snivel → surge → full flight (AoA at trim)
- **Max canopy AoA typically 60-90°**, takes ~4s to settle to trim
- **Physical inflation fast (~1.5s)** but aerodynamic response (AoA settling) much slower
- **Full flight** = canopy AoA reaches trim condition

## Architecture Decisions
- Separate `DeployReplayState` wrapping `CanopyState` (not extending CanopyState directly)
- Deploy sub-phases: `pre_deploy | pc_toss | bridle_stretch | line_stretch | max_aoa | snivel | surge | full_flight`

- Side panel gets "Deployment Replay" section between flight mode and aerodynamics

## Evening Session — Deploy Rendering Polish & Canopy Transparency

### Aero Arrow Deploy Scaling (correct approach)
- **Wrong**: Scaling arrow *lengths* by deploy fraction — forces don't change, canopy is rigid body
- **Right**: Scale segment *positions* horizontally (NED X=chord, Y=span) by deploy fraction
- Matches GLB canopy `scale.set(h, 1, h)` — both axes of horizontal plane
- `deployFraction` property on `GPSAeroOverlay` — wired from deploy timeline in both scenes
- Commits: `7725378` (span only), `fdd2a38` (add chord axis)

### Canopy GLB Transparency
- Added 65% opacity + `depthWrite = false` to canopy GLB meshes so aero vectors show through
- Both inertial and body frame scenes traverse canopy meshes on load
- Canopy scale set to `1.39 * 0.66` — puts vectors at chord plane (between top/bottom skin)
- Hartman note: in real parachute design, lift vectors placed on topskin; drag uses chord plane angles
- Commits: `0e8b5e4` (transparency), `8f55603` (scale 0.7→0.66)

### Line Stretch Detection Improvements
- Peak decel is max *inflation rate* (~1s after line stretch), not line stretch itself
- Pre-line-stretch has near-zero acceleration (freefall)
- Walk backward from 1s before peak, threshold 2 m/s², max 1.5s walk
- PC_TO_LINE_STRETCH: 3.5→1.5s, REACH_GRAB_TIME: 1.0→1.5s (total pre-LS: 3.0s)
- Commits: `ae592cb`, `f3e6633`

### Deployment Roll Smoothing
- Aero-derived roll can flip upside down during deployment (backsliding artifact)
- Circular mean of roll from 2s before PC toss → linear blend toward it during pre-LS phases
- Prevents bad initial conditions for canopy estimator
- Commit: `9f16f06`
