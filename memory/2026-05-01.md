## Thread 2 — Phase F polar tuning

### Methodology established
- **CLI eigenvalue baseline locked first**:
  `npx tsx scripts/eigenvalue-analysis.ts a5segments --save-baseline --no-html`
  → produces `scripts/results/a5segments-baseline.json`.
- Subsequent runs use `--compare` for color-coded deltas across all 7
  airspeeds (25–55 m/s in 5 m/s steps).
- **Caveat acknowledged**: the eigenvalue script runs at neutral
  `defaultControls()` — no throttle, no hipCamber, no legBend. So it
  catches gross stability shifts but can't validate flare authority,
  banked turns, or any throttled config. That requires the browser
  in-the-loop.
- User's actual qualitative validation method: open Debug scenario in
  the polar visualizer, sweep α slider, watch CP arrows + per-segment
  moment arrows. Then fly the model in the gamepad-driven dev server.
  Reported back qualitatively (trim point, stall feel, glide range).
- **Tooling gap noted but not built**: there is no CLI script that runs
  the wingsuit control solver against a GPS track and prints
  convergence% / mean |Δα|. Earlier sessions had eyeballed those numbers
  from the GPS viewer's status panel. Phase F.0 ("build a headless GPS
  smoke test") was offered but user chose to skip and rely on browser
  flying.

### Phase F.1 — Lock baseline ✅
- Saved `scripts/results/a5segments-baseline.json`.
- Reference numbers (neutral controls): trim α 20.77°@25 m/s ↘ 2.63°@55 m/s,
  short-period ζ ≈ 0.14, lateral divergence appears at 40+ m/s (pre-existing
  neutral-config issue unrelated to Phase F changes).

### Phase F.2 — Torso `cp_alpha` 0.025 → 0.05 ✅
- **Physical reasoning**: torso is a low-AR rectangular panel. CP should
  migrate toward area centroid (0.5c) as α grows. Stronger aft drift
  than thin-airfoil 0.025/rad.
- **Result**: invisible to the user; eigenvalue compare showed no
  meaningful shift. Done.

### Phase F.3 — Leg `cp_alpha` sign flip 0.025 → −0.04 ✅
- **Physical reasoning**: leg-wing is apex-aft (wide LE at hips, narrow
  TE at feet). More area forward of geometric mid → CP migrates
  *forward* with α in linear regime, opposite of torso. The legacy
  +0.025 was carried over from the single-center polar and had the wrong
  sign for an apex-aft planform.
- **Result**: large trim shift to deeper α (the old sign was hiding the
  lift-arm imbalance via fake aftward CP drift on the aft segment). User
  countered in the visualizer by setting `hipCamber = 0.73` and
  `legBend = 0.33` at neutral, recovering ~90-93 mph trim.

### Phase F.4 — Leg `cm_0` 0 → −0.03 ✅
- **Physical reasoning**: cambered surface from leg curvature + slight
  reflex from toe-back posture. Thin-airfoil estimate
  cm_0 ≈ −0.6 · (camber/chord). Restores natural pitch-down moment the
  leg-wing should contribute pre-input.
- **Result (with F.2+F.3)**: pendulum qDot grew slightly nose-down at
  every speed (35 m/s: −7.85 → −8.73 rad/s²). Phugoid damping ratio
  jumped substantially (35 m/s: 0.41 → 0.82). Short-period barely moved
  (geometric pitch stiffness preserved). No new instabilities. The
  high-speed lateral divergence was unchanged — that's a separate issue.

### Phase F.5 — Inner+outer wing `s1_fwd` 3.7 → 2.5 ✅
- **Trigger**: F.2-F.4 left the system stall feeling soft. User asked
  whether torso, leg, or arm wings should reclaim sharpness.
- **Recommendation**: **arm wings** (inner + outer), not torso, not leg.
  - Body & leg are bluff/triangular-apex-aft → physically *should* mush.
    Sharpening them would model them as wings, which they aren't.
  - Inner/outer wings are the closest thing in the model to real
    airfoils. Real wingsuit stall is the panels detaching, not the body.
  - Tip stall arrives first traditionally — keeping inner and outer in
    lockstep gives a coordinated break.
- **Result**: trim α virtually unchanged (drift < 0.3°), short-period
  and Dutch roll picked up ~10% damping at low speeds (sharper Kirchhoff
  → more lift loss right at high-α trim → more pitch/yaw stiffness).
  **Bonus surprise**: the high-speed trim that previously needed manual
  hipCamber compensation locked in much better. User reported the
  entire trim range now feels reasonable.
- **Stray-edit gotcha**: first attempt at this commit accidentally
  deleted the newline before `controls: {` on inner-wing polar. Caught
  by tsc. Fix verified before continuing.

### Open follow-ups (not done today)
- **Slow-speed yaw throttle authority**: weak. Yaw throttle drives
  leg/torso roll differential (`YAW_LEG_ROLL_DEG = 12°`), and the
  resulting yaw moment scales with `q·S·c` ∝ V². So at low V the lever
  vanishes. Real fix probably needs a direct cn-style derivative that
  doesn't require sideslip first — but user explicitly parked this.
- **High-speed lateral divergence at neutral controls**: persistent
  through all of Phase F. Not introduced by these changes. Probably
  needs a small dihedral/posture term that real pilots provide
  implicitly. Out of scope for stall tuning.
- **Phase D.2 leg `cm_alpha = 0.6` flare crutch**: stayed off. If flare
  authority feels weak in flight, could revisit as Phase F.7.

## Conventions reinforced today

- Moment summation in [aero-segment.ts](polar-visualizer/src/polar/aero-segment.ts)
  is `r_CP × F` **plus** `q·S·c·CM`. They are *additive*. CM carries
  *only* the camber/reflex residual after CP placement. Putting `cm_0`
  back on the leg-wing (Phase F.4) is correct precisely because the leg
  has cambered geometry independent of the lift × lever-arm pitch
  contribution.
- Sliders that participate in physics often have a parallel JS clamp.
  Update both.
- For tuning JSON-persisted features (videoOffset), prefer attaching
  to an existing scope with version bumps over inventing parallel files.
