## Heading Lock Camera Mode

New **Heading Lock** checkbox added to the GPS viewer capture panel
(inertial scene only). When enabled:

- Overrides the inertial camera's azimuth (θ) each frame to face the
  GPS velocity vector (North/East averaged over ±15 samples for smoothness).
- Preserves keyframe pitch (φ) and orbital radius from the active keyframe.
- Guard: only applied when ground speed > 0.5 m/s to avoid snap at standstill.
- Wired to `headingLock` URL param and persisted in `CaptureSessionState`.

**Implementation detail** (NED → Three.js heading):
```
θ = atan2(velE, −velN)
```
Camera is placed behind the pilot (opposite to velocity direction).

## WAKE-DOWNWASH.md (new doc)

Added `docs/WAKE-DOWNWASH.md` — physics write-up covering:
- Why the leg wing and canopy brake panels operate in upstream wake
- Near-field downwash estimate from Prandtl lifting-line theory:
  `ε ≈ k_wake × 2CL / (π × AR)` where `k_wake ∈ [0.3, 0.5]`
- Sideslip dependence (L1/R1 inner wing wake shift at non-zero β)
- Implementation plan for an explicit `downwashCorrection` in
  `aero-segment.ts` (not yet implemented)
