## Beta Equilibrium Solver Created
- `scripts/beta-equilibrium.ts` — solves for steady-state sideslip β from lateral force equilibrium
- Newton-Raphson on vDot(β) = 0 using full segment model via `computeDerivatives`
- Uses coordinated turn assumption: r_steady = g·sin(φ)/V, p ≈ 0
- CLI: `--speed`, `--alpha`, `--phi`, `--sweep-phi`, `--sweep-speed`, positional polar name
- **Key finding: β is small** — 0.1° to 1.7° across full envelope (25–65 m/s, 5–45° bank)
- β scales inversely with V² (more dynamic pressure = less β needed)
- Converges in 2 iterations (cy_beta makes lateral force nearly linear in β)
- Implication: Hartman's current ψ composition is pretty close; β correction is at most ~1.7° at slow speed + aggressive bank. Transient β during roll reversals could be larger.

## Discussion: Improving GPS→Orientation Pipeline
- Hartman wants to improve wingsuit/canopy orientation visualization from GPS data
- His aerodynamic roll angle is really the lift vector lateral tilt — includes yaw effects, not pure roll
- The polar project's segment model provides what CloudBASE didn't have: coupled roll-yaw relationship, Cn derivatives, dutch roll eigenvector (the β/φ coupling ratio)
- **Proposed approach**: At each GPS timestep, use segment model's lateral equilibrium to estimate β, then decompose measured roll into true roll + sideslip → better ψ and body rates
- Steady-state assumption works for most flight; dutch roll eigenvector handles transients
- Vision: fly the sim alongside GPS recording of the real flight for comparison
- **Next step discussed**: Process GPS CSV through the solver to output enhanced CSV with β, corrected ψ, corrected body rates

## Canopy Mode Added to gps-beta-enhance.ts
- `--mode canopy` flag reads canopy normal CSV (canopy position relative to pilot in NED)
- Canopy normal vector = tension line direction = total aerodynamic force minus pilot drag
- **Derives full canopy orientation from geometry**: project airspeed onto plane ⊥ to CN → forward axis, cross product → lateral, extract Euler angles from DCM, α from body-frame airspeed decomposition + trim offset, β from lateral component
- Body rates via inverse DKE on derived Euler angles (same shared function as wingsuit mode)
- CSV schema: `t, V, vN, vE, vD, cnN, cnE, cnD` (+ optional CL, CD, qbar, rho)
- `--trim-offset` defaults to 3° — canopy equilibrium AOA
- Updated `docs/GPS-STABILITY-SCHEMA.md` with canopy normal format and sign conventions
- **Key insight from discussion**: Canopy orientation is MORE directly observable than wingsuit (the pilot hanging on lines IS a force sensor), so no equilibrium solver needed — geometry gives it
- Hartman's method encodes rotation speed and dynamic maneuvers in the CN position geometry — α and all body rates come from the 3D position/velocity of this vector
