- α and β are outputs of body-frame velocity, not inputs to the DCM
- Wind/stability frame (defined by α,β) is a separate rotation FROM body frame

### Pilot Pendulum Gravity — Body Frame Transform
- **Old formula**: `sin(thetaPilot - thetaCanopy)` — only correct near level flight
- **New formula**: Transform gravity into canopy body frame via DCM, then cross product for torque
  - `gx_body = -g * sin(θ_canopy)`
  - `gz_body = g * cos(θ_canopy)`
  - `τ = m * l * (cos(θ_p) * gx_body - sin(θ_p) * gz_body)`
  - Simplifies to: `τ = -m*g*l*sin(θ_canopy + θ_p)` — gravity restores toward absolute vertical
- At deployment (θ≈84°): equilibrium at θ_p≈-84° (pilot hanging straight down)
- At trim (θ≈6°): equilibrium at θ_p≈-6° (nearly aligned with canopy z)

### Evening Session — DCM Canopy IC Revisited

**Goal**: Fix heading flip bug in DCM/Gram-Schmidt approach for canopy deployment IC.

**Attempts & findings**:
1. **Gram-Schmidt (velocity projection)**: z_body=tension, x_body=velocity projected ⊥ tension. Works for horizontal but velocity nearly anti-parallel to tension in steep flight → numerically unstable, tiny perpendicular component flips sign.
2. **84° chord rotation**: Gram-Schmidt gives x_body ≈ airflow (low α). Added 84° rotation around y_body to get high-α deployment attitude. Steep climb worked, steep dive failed.
3. **x_body negation + heading check**: Dot x_body against wingsuit forward, negate if flipped. Issue: negating x flips y (y = z×x), so 84° rotation around flipped y goes wrong direction. Fixed by negating rotation angle when flipped → climb works, dive still gets ψ=180°/φ=-180°.
4. **ψ override = wingsuit heading**: Hartman rejected — canopy orientation must come purely from tension axis + airspeed geometry, not wingsuit attitude.
5. **Euler normalization**: (ψ+π, -θ, φ+π) is NOT a valid equivalent 3-2-1 Euler representation. Made things worse.

**Current approach (in testing)**:
- **Gravity-referenced frame**: z_body=tension, y_body=tension×gravity (spanwise), x_body=y×z (chord in tension-gravity plane). Then 84° rotation for deployment α.
- Avoids velocity projection entirely — gravity is always well-defined
- Only degenerate when tension exactly vertical (rare, falls back to velocity)
- No heading ambiguity since gravity consistently defines "up"

**Key insight from Hartman**: Canopy orientation at line stretch depends ONLY on bag position relative to pilot (tension axis) + airspeed. NOT on wingsuit heading/pitch/roll. The wingsuit heading approach was always wrong in principle — it just happened to look right in common cases.

**Status**: Gravity-referenced approach compiles, awaiting test results at steep climb and steep dive.

### Inverted Pendulum Bug — Final Root Cause
- With `sin(θp - θc)`, stable equilibrium = aligned with canopy z-axis
