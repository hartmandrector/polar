## Corvid 2 Pattern Analysis
- Imported `Corvid 2 Hartman.glb` into Blender — 2190 objects total
- Patterns are **flat 2D sewing patterns** in Rhino mm coordinates (X ~27000, Z ~-47000, Y=0)
- Segment naming: `arm`, `leg`, `miniarm`, `back`, `chest`, `sidebody`, etc.
- Sub-naming: `segment:surface:type:rib_number` (e.g. `leg:top:dest:1`)
- `legrib_1` through `legrib_39` — empty hierarchy nodes (no mesh data)
- Fabric ribs ARE the 3D airfoil cross-sections — they define inflated wing shape
- Key thickness data (Z span of top skin panels in mm):
  - Leg wing: dest:1=333mm/1086mm, dest:2=290mm/911mm, dest:3=202mm/543mm, dest:4=88mm/124mm
  - Arm wing: dest:5=103mm/1256mm (thickest arm), dest:7-11 ~60-90mm/300-400mm
  - Leg wing is ~3x thicker inflation than arm wing
