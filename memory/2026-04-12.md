## Key Insight from Hartman
- Brakes increase AOA (pitch up), front risers decrease AOA (pitch down) — they're opposing directions
- Not necessarily a 4-way solver because you're either going brakes direction or risers direction for pitch
- Brake turns produce yaw; front riser turns are mostly roll
- Transversal model should also be considered for canopy solver (future)
