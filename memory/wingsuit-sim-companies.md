# Wingsuit Simulation Companies

Competitive landscape for wingsuit VR/simulation systems. Updated 2026-04-13.

## Easysee B.V.
- **Website:** https://www.easysee.eu
- **Location:** Hengelo, Netherlands
- **Contact:** Jordi (jordi@easysee.nl)
- **Type:** VR/AR contract dev studio (Unity3D). Builds custom apps for clients.
- **Tech:** Unity3D, Quest/Vive/Pico/Varjo/Index. Won Dutch VR Award 2017 (wireless VR).
- **Wingsuit sim:** Under development or NDA — no public details. String/puppet-style harness with force feedback. Squirrel is in contact with them (as of April 2026).

## JUMP (Limitless Flight)
- **Website:** https://www.limitlessflight.com
- **Type:** Commercialized VR wingsuit experience at physical locations (US).
- **Hardware:** Fixed metal rig (prone position), custom VR helmet, environmental effects (wind). Uses modified Squirrel suits as the physical harness.
- **Experience:** Airplane exit + 2 BASE jumps + parachute + landing. ~10-12 min flight, $95/pass. Scoring system with gates.
- **Note:** Hartman has done this one.

## Make the Jump
- **Type:** Suspended robot rig (~$1M machine) + VR.
- **Note:** Hartman worked here for ~1 year. Different suspension concept from both JUMP and Easysee. CloudBASE-style physics engine. Company had the robot arm that provides full motion feedback.
